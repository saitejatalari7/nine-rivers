# Privacy Policy for Nine Rivers

**Effective date:** 25 September 2026
**Last updated:** 25 September 2026
**Application:** Nine Rivers (Android, package `com.ninerivers.mahjong`)
**Developer contact:** `saiteja.talari@gmail.com`

This is the single authoritative privacy policy for Nine Rivers. The page published at the
project's GitHub Pages site is a rendering of this file. If the two ever disagree, this file
is correct.

---

## 1. Summary

Nine Rivers is a single-player mahjong solitaire game. It has no user accounts, no login, no
analytics or tracking SDK, and no server operated by the developer. The game plays fully
offline.

The developer receives no personal information about you. The only data that leaves your
phone does so through Google's own services, and only in the cases described in section 4.

---

## 2. What is stored on your device

Your progress and preferences are written to a file in the app's own private storage area.
The file is encrypted and carries an integrity check, so it cannot be read or edited outside
the game.

What it holds:

- Stage progress, stars, best score and best stage.
- Spirit Pearls balance and which tile sets and backgrounds you own or have equipped.
- Daily puzzle streak, the date of your last daily puzzle, and how many timed runs you have
  used today.
- Koi sanctuary state, decorations and tile mastery.
- Settings: sound, music, haptics, reduced motion, colour-blind mode, and whether cloud save
  is switched on.
- The board you are part-way through, so a call or a low-memory kill does not cost you the
  game in progress.
- Whether you have bought the ad-free upgrade, and the state used to pace advertising if and
  when advertising is enabled (see section 5).

This file is local. The game contains no code that uploads it anywhere, other than the
optional cloud save described in section 4.2.

---

## 3. What is not collected

The game does not collect, and contains no code to collect:

- Your name, email address, phone number or postal address.
- Your location, whether precise or approximate.
- Your contacts, calendar, photos, files, microphone or camera.
- Your installed applications or browsing history.
- Any analytics, crash-reporting or attribution telemetry.

The only permission the game asks for on its own account is vibration, for the tile haptics.
Network access is present because the Google Play Billing and Google Play Games libraries
require it, and is used for nothing else. The app requests no location, storage, contacts,
microphone or camera permission, and no advertising identifier permission.

## 4. Third parties

### 4.1 Google Play Billing

Purchases are handled entirely by Google Play Billing. You buy from Google, not from the
developer. Google tells the game which products you own, so the game can unlock them; the
developer never sees and never receives your card number, UPI PIN, bank details or billing
address.

Google's handling of a purchase is covered by the
[Google Privacy Policy](https://policies.google.com/privacy).

### 4.2 Google Play Games Services (cloud save) - optional, off by default

The game can back your progress up to Google Play Games Services saved games, so it survives a
reinstall or a new phone.

- This is **off by default**. It only runs after you turn it on in Settings.
- With it on, the game signs in to Google Play Games with the Google account already on your
  phone, and uploads a copy of the progress data listed in section 2 to Google, where it is
  held against your Google account.
- Your purchases, settings and progress remain playable with cloud save switched off. Nothing
  is withheld from you for declining.
- The developer has no access to your Google account, and no way to read your saved game.

### 4.3 Advertising (Google AdMob) - planned, see section 5

## 5. Advertising

**The build being published contains no ad network.** No advertising SDK is bundled, the
`com.google.android.gms.permission.AD_ID` permission is not in the app's manifest, and no
advertising identifier is read or transmitted. If you install this version and never update,
you will see no advertisements at all.

**Advertising is intended, and is expected to arrive in an update.** The developer has
registered with Google AdMob and plans to serve advertising in Nine Rivers. This policy
describes that use now so that it is disclosed before it begins rather than after.

When advertising is enabled:

- Advertisements will be served by **Google AdMob**. Ads will appear as a banner, as
  occasional full-screen interstitials between stages, and as optional rewarded videos that
  you choose to watch.
- Interstitials are paced by the game: none during the first three stages, at least three
  stages and four minutes between any two, and never during a board you are playing.
- AdMob and its partners may read and use the **Android advertising ID** and related device
  and ad-interaction signals to select advertisements, limit repetition, measure performance
  and detect fraud. This is Google's processing, under Google's policies, not the developer's.
  See [how Google uses data from partner sites and apps](https://policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites)
  and the [AdMob and AdSense policies](https://support.google.com/admob/answer/6128543).
- The advertising ID is resettable, and can be deleted, in Android Settings under Privacy.
  Android also offers an opt-out of ad personalisation there, which the game honours because
  it passes the choice straight to Google.
- The one-time **Remove Ads** purchase (Rs 199) permanently removes the banner and the
  interstitials.

Once advertising ships, the app will declare the `AD_ID` permission and the Play Data Safety
declaration will be updated to say that an advertising identifier is collected and shared.
This section will be rewritten from "planned" to "in use", with the date, at that time.

## 6. Children

Nine Rivers is intended for players aged 13 and over and is **not directed to children**. It
is not enrolled in Google Play's Designed for Families programme. The developer does not
knowingly collect any information from a child under 13. If you believe a child has provided
information, write to `saiteja.talari@gmail.com` and it will be dealt with.

Note that the game contains in-app purchases. Google Play's parental controls and purchase
authentication settings are the right way to prevent unintended spending.

---

## 7. Keeping and deleting your data

- **Local data:** uninstalling Nine Rivers, or clearing its storage from Android Settings,
  deletes the save file and everything in it. There is no copy left behind, and nothing for
  the developer to delete on your behalf.
- **Cloud save data:** saved games belong to your Google account, not to the developer. Delete
  them yourself from the Play Games app (your profile, then Settings, then delete individual
  games or your whole Play Games account), or by removing the game's data from your Google
  account. Turning cloud save off in the game stops further uploads but does not by itself
  remove what is already stored with Google.
- **Purchase records:** your order history is held by Google Play. Requests about it go to
  Google.

Because the developer holds no personal data about you, there is nothing to export or erase
on the developer's side. Requests under India's Digital Personal Data Protection Act, the
GDPR or the CCPA can still be sent to `saiteja.talari@gmail.com` and will be answered.
