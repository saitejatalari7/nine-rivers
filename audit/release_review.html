Nine Rivers - release review

HEAD 0b7e888 · Godot 4.6.2 · 2026-09-21 · every claim below is marked measured (I ran it) or inferred (I read it).

DO NOT SHIP.

One blocker is unambiguous: the back-press diagnostic banner is switched on at HEAD, so every player sees a white debug overlay on their first back press. Two more will be hit in normal play — backing out of Settings from the pause menu abandons the board in every mode, and Shuffle strips Winter Frost, melting half the ice for free in three taps.

Separately, two things that landed today quietly do nothing: the new tile lighting is destroyed by the first clear_board() and never runs in the game, and test_runner.tscn (139 assertions, the project's main regression suite) now crashes six checks in and hangs. The six claimed fixes themselves all hold up — I reproduced each one, including against the pre-fix code.

5 · What I could not verify

  • Anything on a physical Android device. Safe-area insets, whether the back gesture actually arrives, predictive back, INR pricing and Play billing all need OS.has_feature("mobile") or a real handset. get_safe_insets() returns Vector2.ZERO off mobile, so none of my captures exercise the notch path — the full-bleed Settings and Level Select cards may or may not clear a cutout.
  • The “KEY_BACK is only mapped for gamepads” claim. Needs Godot's Android keymap source or a device.
  • test_runner.tscn's 139 assertions. Blocked by finding 5 — the suite cannot complete, so today's tile, save and HUD changes have no coverage from it.
  • ui_layout_audit.tscn. Not re-run; declared flaky and its complaints are 2–4 px price-label overruns. I judged the 17 seal layouts from captures instead.
  • bot_runner. Not run — long, and its River Jade drift is known.
  • Whether restoring the tile lights (finding 4) actually looks good. Nobody has seen that feature on a real board yet, including the commit that added it.

The harnesses and git worktrees used for this review have been deleted; the working tree is clean apart from audit/shots/, which holds the captures this page refers to.
