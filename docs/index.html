Privacy Policy for Nine Rivers (九河)

Effective Date: September 13, 2026 • Last Updated: September 13, 2026

Thank you for playing Nine Rivers (九河) ("the Game"). We value your peace of mind and respect your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains our practices regarding user data.

1. Zero Personal Data Collection

Nine Rivers is designed as a tranquil, offline-first zen experience:

  • We do not collect, store, transmit, or sell any personally identifiable information (PII).
  • We do not request your real name, email address, phone number, physical location, contacts, or photos.
  • No user accounts, social media logins, or external registrations are required to play the full game.

2. Local Save Data & Cryptographic Protection

All game progress — including levels unlocked, high scores, flow streaks, Koi Sanctuary customization, and player preferences — is stored strictly locally on your device using encrypted local storage.

  • If you uninstall the app or clear app storage, your local game data will be reset.
  • Your progress is never uploaded to any external or third-party servers without your operating system's explicit cloud backup (such as Google Drive Device Backup or Apple iCloud).

3. In-App Purchases & Advertising

  • In-App Purchases (Google Play Billing): Any optional purchases (such as cosmetic tile themes or Spirit Pearls) are processed directly and securely through the official Google Play Store Billing service. We never receive, process, or store your credit card numbers, UPI PINs, or financial credentials.
  • Ethical Offerings & Advertisements: If you choose to watch an optional rewarded offering to earn in-game pearls or props, advertisements are served through standard, compliant mobile ad networks (such as Google AdMob). These networks may process non-personal device identifiers strictly to deliver the ad, prevent fraud, and comply with applicable store privacy standards. Full-screen ads are strictly frequency-capped to protect the calm player experience.

4. Children’s Privacy & Family Compliance

Nine Rivers does not target children under 13, nor do we knowingly collect any personal information from children. Because our game collects zero personal data, it is safe, peaceful, and compliant for family and all-ages play.
