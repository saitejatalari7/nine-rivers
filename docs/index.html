Official Policy

Privacy Policy for Nine Rivers

Effective 25 September 2026 • Last updated 26 September 2026
Nine Rivers for Android, package com.ninerivers.mahjong

1. Summary

Nine Rivers is a single-player mahjong solitaire game. It has no user accounts, no login, no analytics or tracking SDK, and no server operated by the developer. The game plays fully offline.

The developer receives no personal information about you. The only data that leaves your phone does so through Google's own services, and only in the cases described in section 4.

2. What is stored on your device

Your progress and preferences are written to a file in the app's own private storage area. The file is encrypted and carries an integrity check, so it cannot be read or edited outside the game. It holds:

  • Stage progress, stars, best score and best stage.
  • Your Spirit Pearls balance, and which tile sets and backgrounds you own or have equipped.
  • Daily puzzle streak, the date of your last daily puzzle, and how many timed runs you have used today.
  • Koi sanctuary state, decorations and tile mastery.
  • Settings: sound, music, haptics, reduced motion, colour-blind mode, and whether cloud save is switched on.
  • The board you are part-way through, so a call or a low-memory kill does not cost you the game in progress.
  • Whether you have bought the ad-free upgrade, and the state used to pace advertising (see section 5).

This file is local. The game contains no code that uploads it anywhere, other than the optional cloud save described in section 4.2.

3. What is not collected

The game does not collect, and contains no code to collect:

  • Your name, email address, phone number or postal address.
  • Your location, whether precise or approximate.
  • Your contacts, calendar, photos, files, microphone or camera.
  • Your installed applications or browsing history.
  • Any analytics, crash-reporting or attribution telemetry.

The only permission the game asks for on its own account is vibration, for the tile haptics. Network access is used by Google Play Billing, Google Play Games and Google AdMob, and for nothing else. The app requests no location, storage, contacts, microphone or camera permission. The Google Mobile Ads library adds the advertising identifier permission (AD_ID), described in section 5.

4. Third parties

4.1 Google Play Billing. Purchases are handled entirely by Google Play Billing. You buy from Google, not from the developer. Google tells the game which products you own, so the game can unlock them; the developer never sees and never receives your card number, UPI PIN, bank details or billing address. Google's handling of a purchase is covered by the Google Privacy Policy.

4.2 Google Play Games Services (cloud save) - optional, off by default. The game can back your progress up to Google Play Games Services saved games, so it survives a reinstall or a new phone.

  • This is off by default. It only runs after you turn it on in Settings.
  • With it on, the game signs in to Google Play Games with the Google account already on your phone, and uploads a copy of the progress data listed in section 2 to Google, where it is held against your Google account.
  • Your purchases, settings and progress remain playable with cloud save switched off. Nothing is withheld from you for declining.
  • The developer has no access to your Google account, and no way to read your saved game.

4.3 Advertising (Google AdMob) - in use; see section 5.

5. Advertising

Nine Rivers shows advertisements, served by Google AdMob, from 26 September 2026.
  • Two kinds of ad: occasional full-screen ads between stages, and optional rewarded videos that you choose to watch for a small in-game reward. There is no banner.
  • Full-screen ads are paced by the game: none before stage 4, at least three stages and four minutes between any two, none for fifteen minutes after a purchase, and never during a board you are playing.
  • Rewarded videos are only ever shown when you tap to watch one, at most four a day.
  • Ad content is limited to Google's PG rating.
  • Players in the European Economic Area and the United Kingdom are shown Google's consent form before any ad is requested, and can choose personalised or non-personalised ads.
  • AdMob and its partners may read and use the Android advertising ID and related device and ad-interaction signals to select advertisements, limit repetition, measure performance and detect fraud. This is Google's processing, under Google's policies, not the developer's. See how Google uses data from partner sites and apps and the AdMob and AdSense policies.
  • The advertising ID is resettable, and can be deleted, in Android Settings under Privacy. Android also offers an opt-out of ad personalisation there, which the game honours because it passes the choice straight to Google.
  • The one-time Remove Ads purchase (Rs 199) permanently removes the full-screen ads. Rewarded videos stay available because you only ever see one by choice.

6. Children

Nine Rivers is intended for players aged 13 and over and is not directed to children. It is not enrolled in Google Play's Designed for Families programme. The developer does not knowingly collect any information from a child under 13. If you believe a child has provided information, write to the address in section 9 and it will be dealt with.

Note that the game contains in-app purchases. Google Play's parental controls and purchase authentication settings are the right way to prevent unintended spending.

7. Keeping and deleting your data

  • Local data: uninstalling Nine Rivers, or clearing its storage from Android Settings, deletes the save file and everything in it. There is no copy left behind, and nothing for the developer to delete on your behalf.
  • Cloud save data: saved games belong to your Google account, not to the developer. Delete them yourself from the Play Games app (your profile, then Settings, then delete individual games or your whole Play Games account), or by removing the game's data from your Google account. Turning cloud save off in the game stops further uploads but does not by itself remove what is already stored with Google.
  • Purchase records: your order history is held by Google Play. Requests about it go to Google.

Because the developer holds no personal data about you, there is nothing to export or erase on the developer's side. Requests under India's Digital Personal Data Protection Act, the GDPR or the CCPA can still be sent to the address below and will be answered.

8. Changes to this policy

Material changes, including the day advertising is switched on, will be published here with a new "last updated" date before or at the time the change reaches players.
