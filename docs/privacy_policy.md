# Privacy Policy for Nine Rivers (九河)

**Effective Date:** September 12, 2026  
**Last Updated:** September 12, 2026  

Thank you for playing **Nine Rivers (九河)** ("the Game"). We value your peace of mind and respect your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains our practices regarding user data.

---

### 1. Zero Personal Data Collection
Nine Rivers is designed as a tranquil, offline-first zen experience.  
- **We do not collect, store, transmit, or sell any personally identifiable information (PII).**
- We do not request your real name, email address, phone number, location, contacts, or photos.
- No user accounts or social logins are required to enjoy the full game.

---

### 2. Local Save Data
All game progress — including levels unlocked, high scores, flow streaks, koi pond customisation, and game preferences — is stored **strictly locally on your device** (via secure local file storage).  
- If you uninstall the app or clear app storage, your local game data will be reset.
- Save data is never uploaded to external servers without your explicit operating system backup (e.g., Apple iCloud Backup or Google Drive Backup).

---

### 3. In-App Purchases & Advertising
- **In-App Purchases (IAPs):** Any optional purchases (such as cosmetic themes or Spirit Pearls) are processed directly and securely through official platform providers (**Apple App Store** or **Google Play Store**). We do not receive or store your credit card or financial credentials.
- **Rewarded Advertisements:** If you choose to watch an optional rewarded offering to earn in-game pearls or props, advertisements are served through standard, compliant mobile ad networks. These networks may process non-personal device identifiers strictly to deliver the ad and prevent fraud, in accordance with applicable store privacy standards.

---

### 4. Children’s Privacy
Nine Rivers does not direct services to children under 13, nor do we knowingly collect any information from children. Because our game collects zero personal data, it is safe and compliant for family and all-ages play.
